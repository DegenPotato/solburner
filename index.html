Reclaim the SOL locked in your empty token accounts

Every Solana token account holds a rent deposit: about 0.0015 SOL for one opened today, about 0.002 SOL for an older one. When its balance reaches zero the account stays open and the deposit stays locked. SOLBurner finds those accounts and closes them in one signature, returning the SOL to your wallet. Non-custodial: you sign every transaction, and recovered SOL goes straight to you.

Questions

Why is there SOL locked in my wallet?

Every token account holds a rent deposit: about 0.0015 SOL for one opened today, about 0.002 SOL for an older one opened under the earlier, higher rate. When you sell or receive a token, an account is opened for it, and when its balance reaches zero the account stays open, still holding the deposit. Closing it returns that SOL to you.

Is it safe to connect my wallet?

The scan is read-only. When you recover, the app closes accounts back to your own wallet, burns only tokens you choose to burn, claims Pump.fun rewards to your wallet if you select them, and adds the SOL transfer for the service fee. It never asks for approvals, delegations or authority changes. Every transaction is simulated before you sign, and you can check it in your wallet's preview. The source code is public, with the full list of instructions.

What does it cost?

0.87% of the SOL recovered, plus Solana's normal network fee. The exact fee and the amount you keep are shown before you sign.

Can I do this for free?

Yes. Solana lets you close token accounts yourself, one at a time, with no service fee. SOLBurner is for doing many at once in a single signature.

Which accounts get closed?

Empty accounts are pre-selected. Accounts that still hold tokens are only closed if you choose to burn those tokens first.
